# Training modules

The 12 training modules in the learning library, what each one covers, and when the app
recommends it. Modules are available in English, Hindi and Tamil. Any limits quoted are
**assumptions we chose for this demo, not Caterpillar specifications.**

## How does the app decide which training to recommend?

The app looks at your last 7 days of work and suggests at most 2 modules at a time, always with the
reason ("You idled 31% last week"):

| What the app sees (last 7 days) | Module suggested |
|---|---|
| Idle time above 25% | Cutting idle time (TM-IDLE-01) or Fuel-efficient operation (TM-FUEL-01) |
| 3 or more harsh manoeuvres | Smooth controls (TM-SMTH-01) |
| More than 20% slower than expected on a task type | Excavator trenching technique (TM-EXC-01) or Loading, hauling and grading cycles (TM-CYC-01) |
| 2 or more proximity breaches | Blind spots and proximity (TM-SAFE-01) |
| 1 or more seatbelt violations | Seatbelt and ROPS (TM-SAFE-02) |
| 1 or more tip-risk events | Working on slopes (TM-SAFE-03) |
| Grouped with "needs safety coaching" operators | Safety scenario simulator pack (TM-SIM-01) |

Recommendations are there to help, not to punish. You can accept or dismiss them.

## What is the "Fuel-efficient operation" module (TM-FUEL-01)?

**Format:** video, 12 minutes, beginner. **For:** all machine types.

Covers how engine speed, power mode and cycle design change fuel use: choosing economy mode,
keeping swing and travel short, avoiding holding the relief valve, and how a clean radiator and air
filter save fuel. It explains the "fuel per productive hour" number in your weekly report and shows
before-and-after examples of the same task done two ways.

## What is the "Cutting idle time" module (TM-IDLE-01)?

**Format:** document, 8 minutes, beginner. **For:** all machine types. **Recommended when:** your
idle time is above 25% in a week.

Explains why high-rpm idling (above about 1200 rpm) costs 6–8 litres of fuel per hour and adds
engine hours, when to use low idle and when to shut down (a guide of about 10 minutes of waiting),
why a hot engine still needs a 3–5 minute cool-down, and how to tell your supervisor when you are
waiting on trucks or access so the plan can change.

## What is the "Blind spots and proximity" module (TM-SAFE-01)?

**Format:** video, 15 minutes, beginner. **For:** all machine types. **Recommended when:** you
have 2 or more proximity breaches in a week.

Shows the blind spots of each machine type, how to read the camera sectors (front, rear, left,
right) and the red / orange / clear zones (under 3 m, 3–7 m, over 7 m, widened by 2 m in low
visibility or high fatigue), and what to do when someone enters a blind spot: stop, horn, wait until
you can see them. Also covers swing radius rules, working with a spotter, and staying alert in the cab.

## What is the "Seatbelt and ROPS" module (TM-SAFE-02)?

**Format:** quiz, 5 minutes, beginner. **For:** all machine types. **Recommended when:** you have
1 or more seatbelt violations.

Short quiz on why the rollover protective structure (ROPS) only protects a belted operator, why you
never jump from a tipping machine, how the seatbelt warning works (moving or under load for more
than 5 seconds; critical at 30 seconds), what to do if the belt switch is faulty (code E-520), and
the cab rule of no phone use while the machine is working.

## What is the "Working on slopes" module (TM-SAFE-03)?

**Format:** interactive scenario, 10 minutes, intermediate. **For:** all machine types.
**Recommended when:** you have 1 or more tip-risk events.

Walks through slope situations step by step: loading on a side slope when the machine starts to
lean, what to do when the roll warning stays on. Teaches the tilt limits used by the app (warning
above 15°, critical above 25°), travelling straight up and down slopes, keeping loads low, never
swinging a full bucket downhill, cutting a level bench, and staying in the seat if the machine tips.

## What is the "Smooth controls" module (TM-SMTH-01)?

**Format:** video, 12 minutes, intermediate. **For:** all machine types. **Recommended when:**
you have 3 or more harsh manoeuvres in a week.

Shows feathering levers in and out, combining movements, keeping engine speed steady and planning
the next move. Explains how jerky operation causes pressure spikes and vibration that wear pins,
hoses and tracks, burn fuel, and can unsettle the machine on a slope.

## What is the "Hydraulic system care" module (TM-HYD-01)?

**Format:** document, 10 minutes, intermediate. **For:** all machine types. **Recommended when:**
your machine shows repeated unusual-behaviour alerts.

Covers the daily hydraulic walk-around (hoses, fittings, cylinder seals, oil level), the meaning of
hydraulic and heat warnings (E-360 low hydraulic oil level, E-365 high hydraulic oil temperature,
the sudden pressure drop warning, and engine overheating E-110 under heavy load), the graded
response (warn, derate, safe shutdown, escalate), and how to tell a one-minute sensor glitch from a
real fault. Stresses the rule: never feel for a hydraulic leak with your hand.

## What is the "Managing fatigue on night shifts" module (TM-FAT-01)?

**Format:** video, 8 minutes, beginner. **For:** all machine types. **Recommended when:** your
fatigue level reaches high.

Explains how the cab camera measures fatigue (eye closure, yawning, head nodding, plus hours into
shift and night shift), why near misses rise late in night shifts, and what actually helps: sleep
before the shift, planned breaks, water and light food, and stopping when tired. What doesn't help:
open windows, loud music, strong tea while working. Your camera frames are never stored.

## What is the "Excavator trenching technique" module (TM-EXC-01)?

**Format:** 60-minute instructor session, advanced. **For:** excavators only. **Recommended
when:** your trenching or digging tasks take more than 20% longer than expected.

A hands-on session with an instructor on the machine: setting up square to the trench, digging to
grade in fewer passes, spoil placement away from the edge, truck positioning for a short swing, and
working safely near trench walls and buried services.

## What is the "Loading, hauling and grading cycles" module (TM-CYC-01)?

**Format:** video, 14 minutes, intermediate. **For:** wheel loaders, dozers and articulated trucks.
**Recommended when:** your loading, hauling, grading or backfilling tasks take more than 20% longer
than expected.

Covers efficient cycles for each machine: the "V" loading pattern for wheel loaders, slot dozing and
smooth grading for dozers, and truck spotting, queuing and haul road flow for articulated trucks.

## What is the "Haul road discipline" module (TM-TRK-01)?

**Format:** quiz, 6 minutes, intermediate. **For:** articulated trucks. **Recommended when:** you
have overspeed events.

Quiz on haul road speed limits and speed-limited zones, following distance, right of way, using the
retarder downhill, never turning sharply at speed or travelling with the body raised, dumping on
level ground, and what to do in rain and dust.

## What is the "Safety scenario simulator pack" (TM-SIM-01)?

**Format:** interactive scenarios, 20 minutes, intermediate. **For:** all machine types.
**Recommended when:** your weekly safety pattern groups you with operators who need safety coaching.

Twelve "What would you do?" situations, each with a picture, 3–4 choices and an explanation: a person
in the blind spot, overheating while lifting, a slope warning, fatigue late in a night shift, a
hydraulic leak, the seatbelt alarm, phone use, entering a no-go zone, rain on a haul road, low
visibility from dust, a sensor glitch versus a real fault, and when to press SOS. Each scenario
links to the module that teaches that topic in more depth. Cheaper than a 3D simulator and still
interactive.
